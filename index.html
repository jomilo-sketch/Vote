BOUESTI SUG Election Rules

Please read the rules below. Scroll to the end to unlock the checkbox, then accept to continue to identity verification.

  1. Eligibility. Only currently registered BOUESTI students with a valid matriculation number and an active @bouesti.edu.ng email address may vote in this election.
  2. One vote per student. Each eligible student may cast only one ballot. Attempting to vote more than once may lead to disqualification of the ballots involved.
  3. Ballot secrecy. Your identity is verified separately from your ballot. Once submitted, no one — including election officials — can trace your selections back to you.
  4. No vote buying or canvassing. Offering, requesting, or accepting money, gifts, or favours in exchange for a vote is strictly prohibited and will be reported to the Electoral Committee.
  5. No impersonation. Voting with another student's matriculation number, password, or email address is a serious offence and may lead to disciplinary action.
  6. Accurate information. You are responsible for entering only your own matriculation number and university email. False information may invalidate your ballot.
  7. Confidential OTP. Your one-time verification code is private. Never share it with anyone, including persons claiming to be election officials.
  8. Final submission. Once you submit your ballot, it cannot be edited, withdrawn, or resubmitted, so review your choices carefully before confirming.
  9. Reporting concerns. If you experience a problem or witness misconduct during voting, report it to the Electoral Committee through the "Need help?" button or official BOUESTI SUG channels.

Need help voting?

Tell the Electoral Committee what went wrong and they will look into it. Please do not include your password or verification code.
